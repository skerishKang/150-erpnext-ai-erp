# First Customer Profile — Ideal Pilot Customer

## Profile Summary

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Industry | Trading, wholesale, light manufacturing, or distribution |
| Size | 5–30 employees |
| Current tools | Ecount or Excel for operations |
| Pain level | Medium to high — manual processes cause daily friction |
| Decision-maker | CEO (often also handles operations directly) |
| Accounting | Uses an external accountant or accounting firm |
| Budget | Willing to pay ₩200,000–₩500,000/month for a business system |
| Tech comfort | Comfortable with web-based tools; does not need on-premise |

## Detailed Profile

### "Kim — CEO of a Trading Company"

**Company:**
- 15 employees
- Imports and distributes industrial components
- 200+ items in catalog
- 30–50 quotations per month
- 10–20 suppliers
- Annual revenue: ₩5B–₩20B

**Current situation:**
- Uses Ecount for basic operations but finds it slow and complex
- Many processes still in Excel (quotations, inventory tracking)
- CEO spends 1–2 hours daily checking orders, inventory, and receivables
- Accountant receives monthly Excel exports for tax filing
- CEO wants to see business status at a glance but lacks the tooling

**Pain points (ranked):**

1. **"I don't know today's sales until someone tells me"** — No real-time visibility
2. **"Quotation takes too long"** — Manual drafting, checking prices, formatting
3. **"Inventory is a black box"** — Stock levels checked manually, often incorrect
4. **"Receivables tracking is manual"** — Who paid, who hasn't, how overdue
5. **"Monthly reporting to accountant is tedious"** — Excel exports, formatting, reconciling

**What they want:**
- One dashboard that shows: orders today, pending quotations, low stock, overdue payments
- Faster quotation creation
- AI to summarize daily operations
- Clean export for accountant

**What they do NOT want:**
- Another tool that adds complexity
- To replace their accountant
- To manage servers or infrastructure
- Long implementation timeline

## Why This Profile is Ideal for Pilot

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Clear pain | Manual processes are already a recognized problem |
| Decision-maker access | CEO makes the purchase decision directly |
| No accounting dependency | Uses external accountant — no expectation of in-system tax filing |
| Ecount user | Understands ERP value; comparison is favorable |
| Right size | Not too small (no budget) and not too large (complex requirements) |
| AI value is clear | CEO briefing and AI quotation directly address top pain points |
