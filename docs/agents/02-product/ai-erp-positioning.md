# AI ERP Positioning

## The Distinction

**AI ERP** is not the same as **AI automation**.

| Aspect | AI Automation | AI ERP (Padiem) |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Core product | Automation platform | ERP system |
| AI role | AI is the product | AI serves the ERP |
| Data source | User provides data each time | ERP holds all business data |
| Value proposition | "Automate any workflow" | "Run your business smarter" |
| Customer relationship | Tool user | ERP customer |

## Why This Matters

### AI Automation (What We Are NOT)

- Generic AI that connects to various APIs
- Users build their own workflows
- Data lives outside the AI
- Value = automation speed
- Example: "Use AI to send emails, update spreadsheets, post to Slack"

### AI ERP (What We ARE)

- ERP system with AI built into workflows
- Workflows are predefined and business-specific
- Data lives inside the ERP
- Value = business intelligence from connected data
- Example: "AI reads your sales history and drafts a quotation for a returning customer"

## The Padiem Advantage

Because AI is embedded in ERP:

1. **Context-aware:** AI knows customer history, pricing, inventory, and receivables
2. **Data-connected:** AI can cross-reference across all business modules
3. **Workflow-integrated:** AI actions happen inside business processes, not outside
4. **Trust-building:** AI suggestions are backed by real ERP data

## Messaging

**To customers:**
> "Padiem은 AI 자동화 도구가 아닙니다. AI가 내장된 ERP 시스템입니다."

**To developers:**
