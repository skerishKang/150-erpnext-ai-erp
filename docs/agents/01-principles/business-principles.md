# Business Principles

## Core Rule

Customers pay for **ERP**, not generic automation.

## Principles

### 1. AI Must Be Embedded Inside ERP

AI features exist to make ERP workflows faster and smarter.
AI does not exist as a standalone product layer.

### 2. First Customers Are Korean SMEs

The initial target is small and medium enterprises in Korea that:
- Currently use Ecount, KSystem, or similar Korean ERP
- Still suffer from manual work despite having ERP
- Need better quotation, inventory, delivery, and receivables management

### 3. Solve Real ERP Pain Points

Every feature must map to a real ERP workflow problem:
- Manual quotation drafting
- Stock status inquiries by phone or messenger
- Delivery tracking across multiple carriers
- Receivables aging that requires spreadsheet exports
- CEO reporting that requires manual data gathering

### 4. ERP Value Comes From Data Integration

The power of ERP is that all business data connects.
AI must leverage this connected data — not just process individual documents.

### 5. Korean Business Context Matters

- Korean accounting conventions
- Korean tax structure (VAT, withholding)
- Korean business document formats (견적서, 거래명세서, 세금계산서)
- Korean communication norms (카카오톡, 문자, business Korean)
