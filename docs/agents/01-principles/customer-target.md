# Customer Target

## Primary Customers

Korean small and medium enterprises (SMEs) with 5–100 employees.

## Target Industries

| Industry | Key Pain Points |
|----------|----------------|
| Manufacturing | Quotation management, inventory tracking, production planning |
| Distribution | Stock management, delivery tracking, receivables management |
| Construction | Project-based quotation, material procurement, progress billing |
| Logistics | Delivery scheduling, carrier management, shipment tracking |
| Installation | Field work scheduling, parts inventory, service completion tracking |
| Field Operations | Mobile work orders, on-site reporting, technician dispatch |

## Customer Characteristics

- Currently using Ecount, KSystem, 더존, or similar Korean ERP
- Still relying on manual processes for key workflows
- Using Excel spreadsheets alongside their ERP
- Communicating business information via phone, messenger, or email
- Spending significant time on quotation, stock inquiry, and reporting

## Pain Points We Solve

1. **Quotation:** Manual drafting, copy-paste from catalogs, no version tracking
2. **Stock:** Phone calls to warehouse, no real-time visibility
3. **Delivery:** Manual tracking across carriers, no unified view
4. **Receivables:** Spreadsheet-based aging, manual follow-up
5. **Reporting:** Manual data gathering for CEO briefings and weekly reports

## Customer Language

- All UI and communication in Korean
- Business documents in Korean format
- Support for Korean accounting terminology
