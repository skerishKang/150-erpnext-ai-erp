# Product Identity

## What This Is

**Padiem AI ERP** is an ERP system.

- It is built on **ERPNext**.
- It is **not** a generic automation tool.
- It is **not** a chatbot wrapper.
- It is **not** a workflow automation platform.
- The product identity is **ERP**.

## What "AI ERP" Means

AI is embedded inside ERP workflows. The AI serves the ERP — not the other way around.

Examples of AI inside ERP:
- AI drafts a quotation from natural language input
- AI generates a CEO daily briefing from ERP data
- AI answers questions about inventory, receivables, or delivery status
- AI prepares accountant-ready document packages

## What "AI ERP" Does NOT Mean

- A generic AI assistant that happens to connect to an ERP
- An automation platform where users build AI workflows
- A chatbot that replaces the ERP interface
